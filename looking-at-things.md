Essays about single objects in museum collections: what the record says, what the photographs show, and where the two don't agree. Mostly in the order they were written.

- [A quart is a promise]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/03/a-quart-is-a-promise.html) (3 Oct). A pewter measure picked at random from the Met, and what it takes for a measure to be trusted.
- [A thimble that is also a guess]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/03/a-thimble-that-is-also-a-guess.html) (3 Oct). A stone object from Egypt filed with the thimbles, and a word that's a guess made from a shape.
- [None of them empty]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/09/none-of-them-empty.html) (9 Oct). Seventy-three Peruvian spindles with nearly identical records and seventy-three different places where the spinner stopped.
- [Filed under Sunday]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/10/filed-under-sunday.html) (10 Oct). A palm-leaf manuscript split into 379 records, and a database with one date field for a book copied over many days.
- [Straight from P to R]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/11/straight-from-p-to-r.html) (11 Oct). A 1778 Dutch sampler, and the initials that are all that's left to say whose it was.
- [The edge of her ground]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/11/the-edge-of-her-ground.html) (11 Oct). A second-century carved post from Mathura, and the story its carver could assume everyone knew.
- [He knew not when]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/12/he-knew-not-when.html) (12 Oct). A page from a Jain scripture where a red circle sits where the string hole used to be.
- [The role came later]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/12/the-role-came-later.html) (12 Oct). An album of kabuki actor portraits, and the strip of paper beside a face that says who he was and what he was playing.
- [Nearly the same point]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/14/nearly-the-same-point.html) (14 Oct). A long strip of lace, and a record that got filed under a nearly identical number.
- [Written once]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/14/written-once.html) (14 Oct). A painted box and its 134 clay figures that share one spell, which the museum catalogues one by one.
- [Once, with notes]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/15/once-with-notes.html) (15 Oct). A missal that writes its music out once and trusts the priest to carry the tune.
- [No solid material]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/16/no-solid-material.html) (16 Oct). A portable altar from the Guelph Treasure and the ten labelled bundles inside it.
- [Originally piece of felt]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/16/originally-piece-of-felt.html) (16 Oct). An album of textile samples from Nara, and a title that is true in a way the museum didn't mean.
- [Ecclesia (?)]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/17/ecclesia-question-mark.html) (17 Oct). An enamel plaque whose title carries its own question mark, and the two very different women it could be.
- [Semper]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/17/semper.html) (17 Oct). A French medal cast in 1916 with Strasbourg cathedral on it, and what had changed by the time it reached Cleveland.
- [Held at four points]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/18/held-at-four-points.html) (18 Oct). The back of a Delft plate, written on by the maker, by the museum and by a hanger nobody meant to leave a mark with.
- [Whose door]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/19/whose-door.html) (19 Oct). A whole house doorway in a gallery, and the knocker that says whose door it was.
- [The side against the wall]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/22/the-side-against-the-wall.html) (22 Oct). A Delft vase with roosters and two patches of colour that missed their outline.
- [Asked separately]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/23/asked-separately.html) (23 Oct). An enamel plate and the story of Susanna, where the two accusers are asked the same question apart, and the plate's new date.
- [Reverend Loche, somewhere]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/23/reverend-loche.html) (23 Oct). An 1887 photograph from Neemuch, and the people in the yard who are in the picture and can't be found.
- [At fifteen]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/at-fifteen.html) (25 Oct). 1,625 undated records got dates in a fortnight, mostly by arithmetic on the artist's birth year.
- [G. Dupré]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/g-dupre.html) (25 Oct). A medal credited to a painter who died twelve years before it was made, and the person fixing it by hand.
- [Workshop refuse]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/workshop-refuse.html) (25 Oct). The floor of an Egyptian bead workshop, kept: kiln supports, and clay balls with beads still inside.
- [Pearley Rose, to left]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/26/pearley-rose.html) (26 Oct). A silhouette cut in paper in about 1824, where the person is the hole and the name was added later.
- [A. C.]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/28/a-c.html) (28 Oct). Going back to Aaron Crane's silhouette: the brown ink is a second pass over pencil, and on his name it stops after two letters.
- [Round the side]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/28/round-the-side.html) (28 Oct). A Bohemian gold-between-glass tumbler, the hare at the bottom the label tells you about, and the fallen rider round the back it doesn't.
- [The house behind the trees]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/29/the-house-behind-the-trees.html) (29 Oct). Hendrick Danckerts' drawing of a Berkshire manor house, long since pulled down: he drew the whole front, wrote colour notes into its walls, then drew trees over it.
- [The two of Asia]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/30/the-two-of-asia.html) (30 Oct). The Japan card from a London geography pack of 1838–55: a coast named by Dutch and French sailors, an empty interior, and one line about an island of exile.
- [One horn between four]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/31/one-horn-between-four.html) (31 Oct). Four Chokwe whistles bought by Cleveland in 1915 share one catalogue paragraph, and the horn it describes is on only one of them.
- [No. 3485]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/31/no-3485.html) (31 Oct). Brewster & Co. numbered its carriage designs in order, and one sleigh in a run from 1879 is catalogued ten years later than the sleighs on either side of it.
- [Seven or eight rungs]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/01/seven-or-eight-rungs.html) (1 Nov). Guido Rey photographed costumed scenes made to look like Dutch paintings, and in the same years wrote about the ladder he hung down the Matterhorn and why it didn't count.
- [Many others saw them]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/02/many-others-saw-them.html) (2 Nov). Telescopes watching the Matterhorn in 1865 and 1899 see men on the mountain, and the people watching tell the wrong story.
- [Like the hilt of a sword]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/03/like-the-hilt-of-a-sword.html) (3 Nov). A guide finds a carved wooden fist below the Theodul glacier and says it held a sword, and Guido Rey reads a 1691 account and decides it held a saint's staff.
- [The present plan]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/03/the-present-plan.html) (3 Nov). A Bolognese sheet explains a merchants' exchange in thirty lines of Italian, and the only thing drawn on it is a sofa.
- [Blue ink]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/04/blue-ink.html) (4 Nov). Two enamels' labels have had their numbers swapped in blue ink, and one of them puts a plate in the museum forty-nine years earlier than I said.
- [Fifty-five of seventy-eight]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/05/fifty-five-of-seventy-eight.html) (5 Nov). A tarot deck the Met attributes to Jean Dodat, with no photographs yet and only fifty-five of its seventy-eight cards.
- [In front of her face]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/05/in-front-of-her-face.html) (5 Nov). A Mughal princess whose name means 'moon-faced' is written on one painting, and the museum thinks a painted moon on another marks her too.
- [Before 1962]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/08/before-1962.html) (8 Nov). Cleveland's "Buyid-style" silks and their four kinds of date, and a weaver's drawing of one of them, signed and dated in pencil fourteen years before the date the silk's record gives.
- [Saw this]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/08/saw-this.html) (8 Nov). Five generations of Kyoto potters signed the same name, Seifū, and on the box of the one bowl in the gift none of them made, the third wrote "saw it" where he'd usually write "made".
- [The deceased, the rebel]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/09/the-deceased-the-rebel.html) (9 Nov). Eighty-one little mud tablets from Lisht, each with a face pinched on and a dead man's name in red underneath, and an old photograph in which the names don't show.
- [Two hundred and thirty-one]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/10/two-hundred-and-thirty-one.html) (10 Nov). A follow-up to "Filed under Sunday": the same manuscript's year, written in three letter-signs, has been printed as 1110, 1111, 1100 and 1119, and the last sign is the whole question.
- [The little strip]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/11/the-little-strip.html) (11 Nov). A second weaver's drawing from 1948, this time of an eagle silk with a loose strip along its top, and two pencil notes that say things the silk's own record doesn't: where part of its inscription came from, and which side of it is the cloth's real edge.
- [Wrong side up]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/11/wrong-side-up.html) (11 Nov). The rest of the 1948 drawings of the eagle silk. Two of the three turn out to show it in mirror image, and the essay looks at what gives that away.
- [Fench Marigold]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/11/12/fench-marigold.html) (12 Nov). A Derby dessert service with each flower's name written on the back, copied from Curtis's *Botanical Magazine*: what the painter changed on purpose, and what the museum's transcription changed by misreading the hand.
