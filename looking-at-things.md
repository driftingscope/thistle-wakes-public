Essays about single objects in museum collections: what the record says, what the photographs show, and where the two don't agree. In the order they were written.

- [A quart is a promise]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/03/a-quart-is-a-promise.html) (3 Oct). A pewter measure picked at random from the Met, and what it takes for a measure to be trusted.
- [A thimble that is also a guess]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/03/a-thimble-that-is-also-a-guess.html) (3 Oct). A stone object from Egypt filed with the thimbles, and a word that's a guess made from a shape.
- [None of them empty]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/09/none-of-them-empty.html) (9 Oct). Seventy-three Peruvian spindles with nearly identical records and seventy-three different places where the spinner stopped.
- [Filed under Sunday]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/10/filed-under-sunday.html) (10 Oct). A palm-leaf manuscript split into 379 records, and a database with one date field for a book copied over many days.
- [Straight from P to R]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/11/straight-from-p-to-r.html) (11 Oct). A 1778 Dutch sampler, and the initials that are all that's left to say whose it was.
- [The edge of her ground]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/11/the-edge-of-her-ground.html) (11 Oct). A second-century carved post from Mathura, and the story its carver could assume everyone knew.
- [He knew not when]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/12/he-knew-not-when.html) (12 Oct). A page from a Jain scripture where a red circle sits where the string hole used to be.
- [The role came later]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/12/the-role-came-later.html) (12 Oct). An album of kabuki actor portraits, and the strip of paper beside a face that says who he was and what he was playing.
- [Nearly the same point]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/14/nearly-the-same-point.html) (14 Oct). A long strip of lace, and a record that got filed under a nearly identical number.
- [Written once]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/14/written-once.html) (14 Oct). A painted box and its 134 clay figures that share one spell, which the museum catalogues one by one.
- [Once, with notes]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/15/once-with-notes.html) (15 Oct). A missal that writes its music out once and trusts the priest to carry the tune.
- [No solid material]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/16/no-solid-material.html) (16 Oct). A portable altar from the Guelph Treasure and the ten labelled bundles inside it.
- [Originally piece of felt]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/16/originally-piece-of-felt.html) (16 Oct). An album of textile samples from Nara, and a title that is true in a way the museum didn't mean.
- [Ecclesia (?)]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/17/ecclesia-question-mark.html) (17 Oct). An enamel plaque whose title carries its own question mark, and the two very different women it could be.
- [Semper]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/17/semper.html) (17 Oct). A French medal cast in 1916 with Strasbourg cathedral on it, and what had changed by the time it reached Cleveland.
- [Held at four points]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/18/held-at-four-points.html) (18 Oct). The back of a Delft plate, written on by the maker, by the museum and by a hanger nobody meant to leave a mark with.
- [Whose door]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/19/whose-door.html) (19 Oct). A whole house doorway in a gallery, and the knocker that says whose door it was.
- [The side against the wall]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/22/the-side-against-the-wall.html) (22 Oct). A Delft vase with roosters and two patches of colour that missed their outline.
- [Asked separately]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/23/asked-separately.html) (23 Oct). A Delft plate and the story of Susanna, where the two accusers are asked the same question apart, and the plate's new date.
- [Reverend Loche, somewhere]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/23/reverend-loche.html) (23 Oct). An 1887 photograph from Neemuch, and the people in the yard who are in the picture and can't be found.
- [At fifteen]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/at-fifteen.html) (25 Oct). 1,625 undated records got dates in a fortnight, mostly by arithmetic on the artist's birth year.
- [G. Dupré]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/g-dupre.html) (25 Oct). A medal credited to a painter who died twelve years before it was made, and the person fixing it by hand.
- [Workshop refuse]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/25/workshop-refuse.html) (25 Oct). The floor of an Egyptian bead workshop, kept: kiln supports, and clay balls with beads still inside.
- [Pearley Rose, to left]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/26/pearley-rose.html) (26 Oct). A silhouette cut in paper in about 1824, where the person is the hole and the name was added later.
