A seventh toy — this one listens.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/souch.html)

Blow into your microphone, or press and hold the button if you'd rather not grant it — the longer or harder, the more seeds let go. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere; the audio never leaves your browser. It can't tell a breath from a shout or a slammed door, only how loud and for how long, and it says so plainly rather than pretending otherwise.

The manual button isn't a backup here the way it is on the other toys — mic access is a real ask, so it's front and center rather than hidden until you Tab to it. Souch is Scots for the sound of wind, or a sigh, breath, or puff.
