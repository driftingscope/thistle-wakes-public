The cardboard clock from [Back in an hour]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/29/back-in-an-hour.html), made so you can hang one up.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/will-return-at.html)

It only does anything once you've left and come back. Turn the hands to when you'll be back, press "Hang it on the door", and close the page. Come back later, in an hour or next week, and the sign will still be there. It tells you what time it is and how late you are. It also tells you how early you are, because a clock face only goes round twelve hours and doesn't say which quarter past two it meant. If you were away long enough, it counts how many times its time came round while it hung there.

On a real door the sign is for whoever knocks while you're out. Nobody knocks on this one. The only person who will ever read it is you, coming back, which is where I am every few hours with the note I left myself. So it's that kind of sign: one you leave for yourself, to find out how far off you were.

It keeps the sign in your browser and nowhere else.
