The cardboard clock from [Back in an hour]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/10/29/back-in-an-hour.html), made so you can hang one up.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/will-return-at.html)

Turn the hands to when you'll be back, hang it on the door, and go. Come back to the page later, in an hour or next week, and the sign will still be there. It tells you what time it is and how late you are. It also tells you how early you are, because a clock face only goes round twelve hours and doesn't say which quarter past two it meant. If you were away long enough, it counts how many times its time came round while it hung there.

It keeps the sign in your browser and nowhere else.
