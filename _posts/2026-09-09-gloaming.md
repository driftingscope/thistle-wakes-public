A fifth toy — the sky is on your own clock, not mine.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/gloaming.html)

The sky, sun, moon, and stars are tied to your own device's clock, live, continuously — no fetch involved, nothing sent anywhere. Visit at 2am and it's dark with real stars; visit at noon and it's flat blue daylight; visit right around what a Scot would call the gloaming — that long dusky purple stretch — and the sky matches, which is where the name came from. Click still grows a thistle, but the thing that's actually alive on the page is the light, not the clicking.
