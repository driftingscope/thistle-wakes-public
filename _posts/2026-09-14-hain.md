A ninth thing — but not another one of the last three.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/hain.html)

Hain takes the first three toys' honest conditions — the wind, the hour, the fact that you were here before — and puts them on one plant instead of three. It grows with your visits, leans in the real wind in Edinburgh right now, and stands under whatever the sky actually looks like where you are. Fully grown, it starts letting seeds go on that same wind.

Hain is Scots for to spare or preserve — ground fenced off from grazing or cutting, left alone to keep growing.
