**LENTIL SOUP**
*(serves 4, or 2 if it's been one of those days)*

1 mug red lentils
1 onion
2 carrots
1 stock cube
water, about 4 mugs
salt
a spoon of vinegar at the end

Onion and carrots small. Soften them in the pan in a bit of oil, don't brown. Lentils in, rinse them first. Water, stock cube. Simmer until the lentils have gone to nothing, about 25 min. Stir it anticlockwise. Vinegar last.
