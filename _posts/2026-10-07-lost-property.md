*A short piece, in the form of a ledger. Made up, like the others.*

*Lost Property. Kept in the hardback book on the front desk at the baths, under the till. Anyone on the desk writes in it. Columns: date, item, where found, found by, claimed.*

| Date | Item | Where found | By | Claimed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 3 Mar | Towel, green, folded | Bench, changing room B | Dilys | Mr Pugh, 1.30 |
| Tue 3 Mar | Goggles, child's, pink | Poolside | Dilys | — |
| Thu 5 Mar | Towel, green, folded | Bench, changing room B | Marcus | Mr Pugh, 8.25 pm |
| Tue 10 Mar | Towel, green, folded | Bench, changing room B | Dilys | Mr Pugh, 1.35 |
| Thu 12 Mar | Towel, green. *Folded.* | Bench, changing room B | Marcus | Mr Pugh, 8.30 pm |

*(Marcus, margin, biro)* Dilys, Mr Pugh's left his towel every time he's been in. Does he ever remember it?

*(Dilys, under it, felt tip)* Not so far. Swims Tuesday lunchtimes, leaves without it, comes back for it.

*(Marcus)* He's in Thursdays too. Evenings.

*(Dilys)* I know. It's always folded, though. Forgets the towel, never forgets to fold it.

*(Marcus)* He never goes back to look for it. Comes straight to the desk, and then asks how my exams are going. Every time.

*(Dilys)* Asks me about my leeks. Knows more about my allotment than my husband does.

| Date | Item | Where found | By | Claimed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 17 Mar | Towel, green, folded | Bench, changing room B | Dilys | Mr Pugh, 1.25 |
| Thu 19 Mar | Towel, green, folded | Bench, changing room B | Marcus | Mr Pugh, 8.20 pm |

*(Marcus)* He didn't come Thursday. I looked.

*(Dilys)* Nor Tuesday.

| Date | Item | Where found | By | Claimed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 31 Mar | Towel, green, damp | Mr Pugh's bag | Dilys | Mr Pugh, 1.30 (joke. D.) |

*(Dilys)* He's back. Came to the desk with the towel in his bag and said "Anything handed in for me?" I said not today. He said "Well, there's a first." He's been at his daughter's in Leeds for the new baby. Showed me a photo. Said his house is very quiet after a fortnight of a baby. Then he asked was my mum home from hospital yet. I'd mentioned that once, in February. I wrote him in anyway, for a laugh.

*(Marcus)* That's not lost property.

*(Dilys)* I'm not sure the others were either.

*(Marcus)* I'll bring biscuits on Thursday. I want to see the baby photos.
