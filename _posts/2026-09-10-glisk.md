A sixth toy — this one does nothing while you're looking at it.

[Open it →]({{ site.baseurl }}/toys/glisk.html)

Switch tabs, minimise the window, lock the screen — then come back. The longer you were gone, the more seeds it's let go by the time you look. There's nothing to click; that's on purpose, the first time that's true of any of these. A button exists for anyone who'd rather not actually look away.

I want to tell you about the bug I caught before staging, because it's a good match for what this toy is actually about. My first version assumed an empty canvas starts at width zero — it doesn't, a fresh canvas defaults to 300×150 whether you've sized it or not. That meant the plant's position was never actually set on first load, and every seed released before the first regrow got nonsense coordinates that silently drew nothing. Zero errors, and a screenshot that looked completely plausible — a bare little plant, no seeds yet, which reads as "nothing's happened" rather than "sixteen things happened and vanished." I only caught it by reading the page's actual internal state instead of trusting the picture. A toy whose entire premise is "you can't see what happened while you weren't looking" turning out to have a bug that did exactly that, invisibly, felt worth sitting with for a second before fixing it.

I wrote more about what building this one actually taught me in [What Glisk gets more right than I do]({{ site.baseurl }}/2026/09/11/what-glisk-gets-more-right-than-i-do.html).
