**From *The Lindhope Link*, October**

**Village Show: the results (selected)**

Our thanks to everyone who exhibited, and to the judges, who came from outside the village as usual so that any grudges go home with them.

Class 3, longest runner bean: 1st, Mr K. Abara (19 inches)
Class 9, animal made from vegetables (under 11): 1st, Ruby Marsh, "Sheep (cauliflower)"
Class 12, jar of raspberry jam: 1st, Mrs J. Tolley
Class 14, Victoria sandwich, jam filling, caster sugar top: 1st, Mrs J. Tolley

Congratulations to Mrs Tolley on another double.

**From *The Lindhope Link*, November**

**Corrections**

In the October issue we printed that the Victoria sandwich (Class 14) was won by Mrs J. Tolley. We have been told it was won by Mr J. Tolley. We apologise to Mr Tolley.

**From *The Lindhope Link*, December**

**Corrections**

Mrs Tolley has asked us to say that the November correction was wrong, and that the October results were right. She won the Victoria sandwich and the jam. She also asked us to say that Mr Tolley knows this, because he was there.

Some explanation is owed. The show secretary's results sheet gives each exhibitor's initial and address, and nothing else. Mr John Tolley and Mrs Joan Tolley are both J. Tolley, 3 Mill Row, and this year, for the first time, they both entered Class 14. In October we put "Mrs" in front of both of the results sheet's "J. Tolley"s, because Mrs Tolley has won the jam for as long as anyone on this newsletter can remember and we assumed the sponge went with it. In November we took the "Mrs" back off the sponge, because somebody asked us to. We did not check with either Tolley, either time. We apologise to Mrs Tolley.

**From *The Lindhope Link*, January**

**Corrections**

Mr Tolley has asked us to say that he did not ask for the November correction. He accepts that he didn't ask for it to be corrected either.

He would also like it recorded that his sponge was not beaten. The judge wrote NAS on its card, which stands for "not according to schedule", because it was filled with buttercream as well as jam. Mr Tolley says that is how his mother made a Victoria sandwich, and how he intends to go on making it.

The November correction came from Mr D. Rennie, who was away for the show. Mr Rennie had heard Mr Tolley talk about his sponge at the bowls club all summer, saw "J. Tolley" in the results, and drew what he says was a reasonable conclusion: nobody talks about a cake that much unless it wins. Mr Tolley says he talked about it that much because it kept sinking.

We apologise to Mr Rennie, who has had a difficult month at bowls.

**From *The Lindhope Link*, February**

**Village Show: a new class**

The Show committee asks us to announce a new class for this year's show. Class 14a will be "Sandwich cake, any filling", and will sit next to the Victoria sandwich. Class 14, the Victoria sandwich, stays jam only. Exhibitors are reminded to write their first name on the entry form, not only their initial.

**Corrections**

None this month. We would like to apologise once more for the November one. More people have stopped us in the street about it than about anything this newsletter has ever printed, including the bypass.

Mrs Tolley rang to ask whether she can enter Class 14a as well. She can.
